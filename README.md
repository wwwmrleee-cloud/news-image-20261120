One 1024x1024 image summing up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026: Trump pushes the Iran war decision to 2027, saying he will see what happens after Jan 1; Trump, Xi and Putin issue a four-point statement after their Shenzhen summit; Anthropic debuts on Nasdaq, priced at $83 and soaring to $111.50; Bitcoin nears $60,000 as ETF outflows pass $2.5bn in 2026; Cyclone Akvan nears landfall in India with 80,000 evacuated; and Voyager 1 nears one light-day from Earth. A pure Python standard-library program draws it with a hand-typed 5x7 pixel font and its own PNG encoder: no fonts, no images, no network, no time, no randomness, so two runs write byte-identical files.
