One 1024x1024 image summing up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026: Trump puts the Iran decision off to 2027, saying he will see what happens after Jan 1; Trump, Xi and Putin issue a four-point joint statement in Shenzhen; Anthropic shares soar from $83 to $111.50 on their Nasdaq debut, worth about $1.5 trillion; Bitcoin nears $60,000 at $61,635 as the fear index sits at 10; Cyclone Akvan forces 80,000 people to evacuate ahead of a midday landfall in India; Voyager 1 nears one light-day from Earth; and the ICJ rules on Myanmar and the Rohingya. A pure Python standard-library program draws it with a hand-typed 5x7 pixel font and its own PNG encoder: no fonts, no images, no network, no clock, no chance, so two runs write byte-identical files. The lead story is illustrated with a hand-drawn 2027 January calendar, Jan 1 picked out in red.
